Superweek 2026

The questions your dashboard can't answer

Every analytics team can tell you what happened. Almost none can tell you why, and why is what actually drives good decisions. Two well-placed questions can unlock more than years of behavioral data ever will.

Marketing Unusual: Things we should and shouldn't do

With the shift in marketing and a new reality built on data and AI, choices suddenly become much more important, because both the impact and the risks grow for everyone.

Allweb Digital Analytics Conference

Campaign tracking and attribution: Tips, tricks and tactics

Everyone knows how to do campaign tracking, right? Wrong. There have never been more ways to go wrong. This session dives into the decisions that need to be made.

Helsinki CDOIQ

The Prediction Paradox: Why More Data Makes Leaders Less Certain, And What Actually Changes That

More dashboards should mean more certainty, but for most leaders it does the opposite. This talk unpacks decision laundering, the quiet ritual of using data to justify calls that were already made, and what it actually takes to reverse it.

Data Innovation Summit

Making Data Actionable: A Decisions-First Approach

Making data actionable starts with a decisions-first approach, not a data-first one. Shift the focus from how much data you can gather to who can actually act on it, and the return on that data investment goes up fast.

Fixing Analytics-Driven Marketing Decisions (Unless You Have a Bigger Budget Than You Need)

Most marketing decisions only get real scrutiny when budgets are tight. Give a team unlimited spend and suddenly nobody needs to prove anything works. This talk is about building decision discipline in before the budget forces it on you.
